# Discover Diplomacy — Messaging Guide

Internal reference for consistent terminology across the site, emails, and docs.

## Audiences (four, not three)

1. **Candidates** — individual students and early-career professionals.
2. **Universities** — IR / international affairs / global studies programs buying memberships in bulk.
3. **Employers** — verified organizations hiring in international affairs.
4. **Coaches** — vetted insider mentors who deliver paid sessions.

## Tier vocabulary

| Use this                              | Don't use                                          |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Compass** ($20/mo) — self-directed  | "the cheap tier", "basic membership"               |
| **Envoy** ($150/mo) — mentor included | "premium membership", "advisory engagement"        |
| **University Program** ($20/stu/mo)   | "enterprise", "bulk discount"                      |
| **Resume Drop** / **Member Pool**     | "candidate database", "talent pipeline"            |

Switch decisively. Avoid generic words like "membership", "services", "platform"
unless the broader context is obvious.

## Phrase replacements

| Old phrasing                                | New phrasing                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "membership" / "our memberships"            | "Compass" or "Envoy" or "Compass or Envoy"                            |
| "Join our membership"                       | "Start with Compass" or "Start Envoy"                                 |
| "Start a Membership" (CTA)                  | "Start with Compass"                                                  |
| "services", "advisory practice"             | "the international career platform"                                   |
| "platform"                                  | "the international career platform" or "the talent infrastructure layer" |
| "community", "network" (for candidates)     | "members-only community"                                              |
| "job board", "directory" (in opportunities) | "curated opportunities", "global job board", "weekly digest"          |
| "access vetted candidates"                  | "unlock vetted international talent"                                  |
| "hire talent"                               | "browse pre-screened candidates"                                      |

## CTAs (consistent across pages)

- Candidates: **"Start with Compass"** (primary) · **"See Envoy with Coaching"** (secondary)
- Universities: **"Learn about our University Program"** · **"Request a demo"**
- Employers: **"Browse Vetted Talent"** (primary) · **"See Employer Pricing"** (secondary)
- Coaches: **"Apply to Coach with Us"**

Never use generic "Get Started" — every CTA should name the action.

## Pricing facts

- **Compass:** $20/mo · $192/yr (annual = ~20% off)
- **Envoy:** $150/mo · $1,440/yr
- **University Program:** $20/student/mo, min 50 students
- **Employer Free:** browse public profiles
- **Employer Starter:** $30/mo, 3 unlocks
- **Employer Professional:** $100/mo, 12 unlocks
- **Employer à la carte:** $18 per unlock
- **Placement fee:** 15% of first-year base salary (full-time) · 10% (≤12-month roles)

## Tone

- **Candidates:** warm, action-oriented, no jargon.
- **Universities:** professional, outcomes-driven.
- **Employers:** crisp, businesslike, quality-signaling.
- **Coaches:** mission-forward, respectful of expertise.
